# DnD Session Manager — Player Guide

## Getting in

You log in with Discord, but only once your DM has invited you.

1. Tell your DM your Discord **username** — the unique one without spaces, not your display name.
2. Open [dnd.astralex.dev](https://dnd.astralex.dev) and click **Log in with Discord**. Discord asks once to share your username and avatar, nothing else.
3. If you see *You're not invited yet*, the invite isn't there yet or has a typo — ask your DM to check it.

Your name sits top right; click it to log out.

## Your character

Your character belongs to your account — only you can edit it, and **Characters** in the sidebar opens it directly.

- **No character yet?** Characters shows the list: click **+ Create New Character** and go through the wizard. A character from before logins existed appears once your DM assigns it to you.
- **One active character.** If you have several, **Set Active** in the list picks the one you play; the others become inactive. **All my characters** at the top of the sheet leads back to the list.
- **Editing:** every field saves by itself when you click away (a green note confirms). Skills, saves and inspiration save on click.
- **Level up:** **+ Level Up** at the top. Enter the new level and your hit-die roll; the app adds your Constitution modifier and proficiency bonus.
- **Coin purse:** one counter per coin type under Basics, edited like any field.
- **Inventory:** the sheet lists your items with their weight and the total. **View inventory** opens them as cards: click a card to edit it (it saves when you click away, or with **Done**), **+ New item** adds one. Anything you buy in a shop lands there by itself.
- **Download PDF** gives a print-ready sheet.

## At the table — the Board

The **Board** is the live table: it updates by itself, and **Sheet ›** / **‹ Board** switch between it and your full sheet in one click.

| Panel | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| Character | Your HP and ability modifiers at a glance; **Sheet ›** opens the full sheet. |
| Dice | Pick dice, count and modifier, roll. Rolled real dice? Keep the same selection, type the total and record it (e.g. 5d12+3 → 35). Only you see your last 5 results, at the bottom; ✕ removes one, **Clear all** empties the list. |
| Initiative | The fight order, live. The DM keeps it. |
| Timer | Spell and effect timers the DM runs for everyone or for your character, counting down. |
| Shop | Shops the DM opened for you pop up by themselves, with their location under the name. Items are grouped by category, with weight and description. **Buy** works only if you have exactly the listed coins — too expensive shows red, sold out disappears. What you buy goes straight into your inventory. |
| Maps | Maps shared with you, with a preview. A new one pops up: **View now** or **Later**. 👁 hides it, 🔒 keeps it visible after the session ends, ↪ hands it to another character. |
| My Notes | Your private notes, quick to reach. |
| Calculator | The four basic operations. |

## Your notes

**Notes** in the sidebar is your private notebook: as many titled pages as you like. Pages save with the **Save** button, not by themselves. Nobody else can read them — not even the DM.

## Good to know

- **The DM can read your sheet but can't change it**, and can't see your notes or your dice rolls.
- **Coins never convert:** a price must be paid in exactly the listed coins; the same value in other coins doesn't count.
- **House rule — ability modifiers:** below 10, each point is −1 (9 = −1, 8 = −2, 7 = −3); from 10 up it's standard 5e.
- **House rule — level-up HP:** hit-die roll + Constitution modifier + proficiency bonus.
- **A new character starts from zero:** no maps or shops carry over from an old one.

## Quick answers

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *You're not invited yet* | Give your DM your exact Discord username. |
| No character after logging in | Create one, or ask the DM to assign your old one. |
| A shared map is missing | You may have hidden it: *Show hidden maps* at the bottom of the Maps panel. |
| Buy is greyed out | You lack the exact coins listed — check your purse on the sheet. |
| A page looks out of date | Reload it; nothing is lost. |
| Something is broken | Tell the DM or Severin what you clicked. |
