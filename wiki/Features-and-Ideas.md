# Features and Ideas

## What the tool can do

Explained for the people using it, not as technical documentation, in the two guides:

- **[DM Guide](../docs/guides/DM-Guide.pdf)** — invites, setup before the first session, every Board panel,
  characters, notes and quotes, the rules the tool enforces, quick answers.
- **[Player Guide](../docs/guides/Player-Guide.pdf)** — getting in, your character, the Board, notes, house rules.

Their text lives in [`docs/guides/`](../docs/guides/) as Markdown, with instructions for rebuilding the PDFs.
Release-by-release changes are in the [CHANGELOG](../CHANGELOG.md).

## Ideas for later (not built, just noted)

Every item here is an idea, not a plan. The DM decides what gets built.

- **Currency exchange at the merchant.** A price has to be paid in exactly the listed coins today. A merchant could
  optionally accept another coin, probably at a worse rate — the mechanics aren't settled.
- **Books visible to players.** Books are deliberately DM-only. A second view on the Board could let players read
  chosen books (the Player's Handbook, say). Not decided which books, or whether it's set per book.
- **Several campaigns and game creation.** The schema is ready (see [Data Model](Data-Model.md)); the UI comes with
  the multi-user stage (Pflichtenheft 8.11).
- **Google login next to Discord**, with account linking — after the first full release.
